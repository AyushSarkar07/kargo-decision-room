# Walkthrough script (about 8 minutes)

Open the app. The chips in the header say exactly what is live: database, AI, and email mode.
In demo mode, click **Load synthetic demo applicants** first.

1. **Rubric tab: where the scoring comes from (1 min).**
   "These criteria come from Arjun's eight past hires, not the job descriptions. All five
   Exceeds hires had done operational work themselves; none of the other three had."
   Point to a counterexample (Rahul Bose: independent, rated Meets) and the limitations.
   Toggle PM/SPM to show different weights, each totalling 100%.

2. **Upload tab: the pipeline (1.5 min).**
   Drop two CVs and pick a role for each. Point to the steps panel: personal details are
   removed on the server before anything reaches the AI. Include an unreadable PDF to show
   "Needs text" and the paste-text recovery.

3. **Product Manager tab: the Ten Minute Review (2.5 min).**
   Pick the top candidate. Read the four boxes: why this person, strongest evidence,
   what remains uncertain, what to ask. Note the ranking score, the coverage bar, and the
   other-role score. Click a criterion to open the **Evidence Trail**: the CV line behind
   the score, what that score means, and which past hires the criterion came from.
   Show a candidate marked *Incomplete*: "Not evidenced" is not the same as a low score.
   Point to the line below the top five: "Needs review, not rejected."

4. **Second Look (45 s).**
   People who might be missed: thin CVs, big PM/SPM gaps, the CV with an embedded
   "ignore previous instructions" line, which was flagged and not obeyed.

5. **Decide and send (1.5 min).**
   Before a decision, *Confirm and send* is disabled. Click **Invite**. The invite draft is
   editable; use *Preview with name* to see it with the name filled in. Point to the
   **Delivers to** line: in test mode only allowlisted addresses receive mail. Confirm.
   The dialog shows the real destination. After sending, the decision and draft lock, and
   the status shows "Accepted by Resend" (or "Simulated send"), which is not the same as
   "Delivered".

6. **Sent log (30 s).**
   Every attempt, where it went, the provider id, and its status. A second click cannot send
   twice.

Close with: "The system ranks and explains. Arjun decides, and nothing goes out until he
confirms."
